# Nullfield — a theremin simulator

Play a theremin in virtual reality on Meta Quest: your right hand sets the pitch at the
rod, your left hand the volume over the loop, with no touch at all — just like the real
instrument. Three instruments from a 1929 cabinet to a near-future column, a parlour and
a recording studio, a guided tutorial, play-along pieces, effect pedals and a looper.
